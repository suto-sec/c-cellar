# A receipt

Print the receipt shown below. Columns:

- item name: **left-aligned** in 10 characters;
- quantity: **right-aligned** in 5 characters;
- price: **right-aligned** in 8 characters with two decimals.

The unit prices are 3.50 (coffee, quantity 2) and 2.25 (bread, quantity 1). The `Total` line has no quantity (5 spaces) and shows **2 x 3.50 + 2.25**, which your program must compute.

**Example**

```
$ ./prog
Item        Qty   Price
Coffee        2    3.50
Bread         1    2.25
Total              9.25
```
